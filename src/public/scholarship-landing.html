The Chevening Scholarship is the UK government's international awards programme, funded by the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO). It offers full financial support to outstanding individuals with leadership potential to study any subject at any UK university for one year. Benefits include full tuition, a monthly stipend, travel allowance, and visa fees covered.
